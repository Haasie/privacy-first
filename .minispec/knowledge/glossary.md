# Glossary

*Domain terms and definitions will be added during development.*

## Key Terms

- **PII** — Personally Identifiable Information; any data that can identify a specific individual
- **Redaction** — Replacing PII with a placeholder (e.g. `[NAME]`, `[EMAIL]`) in the output
- **False negative** — PII that was not detected and remained in the output
- **False positive** — Non-PII text that was incorrectly redacted
